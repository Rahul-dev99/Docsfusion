How to split a PDF

Upload one PDF, enter the page ranges you want to separate, and select Split PDF. DocsFusion creates a separate PDF for each valid range you enter.

Split PDF pages by range

You can enter ranges such as 1-3, 4-6, or individual pages such as 8. Each range is created as a separate PDF file.

Why use DocsFusion to split PDFs?

DocsFusion is designed to process supported PDF operations directly in your browser, helping you split documents quickly while keeping the workflow simple.
