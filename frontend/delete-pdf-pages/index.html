How to delete pages from a PDF

Upload one PDF, enter the page numbers or page ranges you want to remove, and select Delete Pages. DocsFusion creates a new PDF without those pages.

Remove individual pages or page ranges

You can remove individual pages such as 2 or 5, or delete several pages at once using ranges such as 7-9. Multiple selections can be combined using commas, for example 2, 4, 7-9.

Keep at least one page

DocsFusion prevents you from deleting every page in the document. At least one page must remain in the output PDF.

Why use DocsFusion to delete PDF pages?

DocsFusion is designed to perform supported PDF operations directly in your browser, helping you remove unwanted pages quickly while keeping the workflow simple.
