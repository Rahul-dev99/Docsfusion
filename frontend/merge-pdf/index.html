How to merge PDF files

Upload your PDF files, arrange them in the order you want, and select Merge PDFs. DocsFusion combines the pages into one PDF that you can download.

Why use DocsFusion to merge PDFs?

DocsFusion is designed to process supported PDF operations directly in your browser, helping you work with documents quickly without unnecessary steps.
