How to rotate PDF pages

Upload one PDF, choose whether to rotate all pages or only selected pages, select the rotation angle, and click Rotate PDF. DocsFusion creates a new PDF with the selected pages rotated.

Rotate specific PDF pages

You can rotate the entire PDF or enter individual pages and page ranges such as 1, 3, 5-7. This is useful when only some pages are scanned or displayed in the wrong orientation.

Rotate PDF pages clockwise or counter-clockwise

DocsFusion supports 90-degree clockwise, 90-degree counter-clockwise, and 180-degree page rotation.

Why use DocsFusion to rotate PDFs?

DocsFusion is designed to perform supported PDF operations directly in your browser, helping you correct page orientation quickly while keeping the workflow simple.
